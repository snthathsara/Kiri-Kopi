# KÍRI KÓPI — Official Web Experience

An editorial, high-end web experience crafted for **KÍRI KÓPI Flower Road**, located at **76/1 Flower Road, Colombo 07, Sri Lanka**.

**Specialty Coffee Shop & Bakery | All-Day Brunch | Artisanal Brews & Pastries | Daily 7:30 AM – 10:00 PM | Walk-Ins Welcome**

---

## ✨ Design & Brand Highlights

- **Signature Light Pastel Yellow & Black-ish Espresso Theme**:
  - Soft pastel butter yellow (`#F6E59A`, `#FAF0B8`, `#FFFDF8`), deep roasted espresso black-ish brown (`#1A120B`, `#291B10`), and warm rich cocoa accents.
  - Seamless Light and Dark modes inspired directly by the official brand logo.
- **Editorial Typography & Visual Hierarchy**:
  - Chunky retro display serif powered by **Fraunces** with stacked repeating typography.
  - "Coffee is a craft, and every cup tells a story" typographic quote ribbon.
  - "IT'S KÓPI O'CLOCK" graphic banner.
  - Transparent iced specialty coffee cutout floating dynamically over the hero headline.
- **Instagram Story Highlights Bar**:
  - All-Day Brunch 🥐, Sandos & Plates 🥪, Specialty Coffee ☕, Matcha & Sips 🍵 with interactive scroll-to triggers.
- **Crafted 4-3-4-3 Menu Structure**:
  - **Mains (4)**: KK Diner Waffle (Rs. 2,850), Smashed Beef Burger (Rs. 2,900), KK Chilli Eggs (Rs. 2,650), Jaggery Beef Croissant (Rs. 2,950).
  - **Snacks & Sandos (3)**: Grilled Cheese in Shokupan [V] (Rs. 2,250), Smoked Salmon Sando (Rs. 2,750), Breakfast Tacos [VO] (Rs. 2,350).
  - **Specialty Coffee & Cold Espresso (4)**: Iced Tin Kiri Kōpi (Rs. 1,180), Tiramisu Latte (Rs. 1,280), KK Cold Foam - Sicilian Pistachio (Rs. 1,450), Orange Cold Brew (Rs. 1,180).
  - **Bakery, Matcha & Refreshers (3)**: KK French Toast (Rs. 2,650), Kyoto Strawberry Matcha Latté (Rs. 1,480), Frozen Naarang Lemonade (Rs. 980).
- **Live Restaurant Status Engine**:
  - Automatically calculates Sri Lanka time (`Asia/Colombo`) and displays real-time open/closed status for operating hours: **Daily 7:30 AM – 10:00 PM**.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Markup & Styling**: Semantic HTML5, Modular Modern CSS (CSS Variables, clamp(), Flexbox, CSS Grid)
- **Tooling & Bundler**: [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Typography**: Google Fonts (Fraunces, Inter, JetBrains Mono)
- **Icons**: Custom Handcrafted SVGs & Lucide
