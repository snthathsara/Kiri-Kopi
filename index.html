From Ella's Hills to Flower Road, Col 7

Where specialty coffee craft meets warm bakery comforting bakes.

Born in the misty mountain landscape of Ella and brought to Colombo’s leafy Flower Road, KÍRI KÓPI was created as an intentional coffee sanctuary. Here, we honor the rich Sri Lankan tradition of milk coffee reimagined through specialty roasts, poured into our signature vintage iced tins. Alongside freshly whisked ceremonial grade Kyoto matcha, artisan sourdough toasties, cassava waffles, and freshly rolled butter croissants, every detail is crafted for slow sips, rich conversations, and relaxed mornings.
